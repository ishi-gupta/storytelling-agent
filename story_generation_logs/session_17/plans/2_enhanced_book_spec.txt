Genre: Contemporary romantic fiction with elements of self-discovery and adventure
Place: Paris in springtime - the enchanting Canal Saint-Martin with its tree-lined pathways and iron footbridges, the artistic neighborhood of Montmartre with its winding cobblestone streets and the stunning view from Sacré-Cœur, the charming Belleville with its vibrant markets and colorful street art, the historic Latin Quarter with its cozy bookshops and sidewalk cafés; opening scenes in Brooklyn, NY showing Maya's everyday life before her transformation
Time: Present day, early spring (March/April), one week that changes everything. Cherry blossoms beginning to bloom, golden morning light, mild temperatures perfect for wandering, spring rain that makes the city sparkle, café terraces coming alive with the season
Theme: The courage to embrace spontaneity; finding yourself in unfamiliar places; how stepping outside your comfort zone leads to unexpected joy and connection; the magic of following your heart; discovering that real life can be even more beautiful than the movies
Tone: Wonderlust and magical realism; romantic and dreamy with moments of genuine emotion; whimsical yet heartfelt; captures the feeling of falling in love with a city and rediscovering yourself
Point of View: First person past tense, richly descriptive and sensory - the smell of fresh croissants, the sound of accordion music drifting through streets, the feeling of cobblestones underfoot, the taste of wine at sunset; lyrical internal thoughts; snippets of French phrases that become meaningful; warm observational humor and genuine wonder
Characters: - Maya, 27, Brooklyn copywriter yearning for adventure beyond her screen; initially cautious but blooms with confidence as she explores; discovers she's braver than she thought; has a gift for connecting with people despite language barriers. - Lila, 28, Maya's supportive best friend and roommate; cheers Maya on from afar via enthusiastic voice messages; helps Maya see her own growth. - Sylvie Martin, 50s, Maya's warm and nurturing Airbnb host; shares insider tips and homemade treats; becomes like a Parisian aunt figure who encourages Maya to embrace the adventure. - Étienne, 30, charming linguistics grad student and neighbor; patient and kind, helps Maya navigate both the language and the city; becomes an unexpected friend and possibly something more. - Camille Laurent, 29, free-spirited photographer and artist; teaches Maya to see Paris through an artist's eyes; brief but meaningful connection that opens Maya's heart. - Samir, 40, wise café owner near Canal Saint-Martin; philosophical conversations over coffee; offers fatherly advice and becomes Maya's favorite breakfast spot
Premise: After watching a beautiful French film about Paris one evening, Maya feels a sudden longing for adventure and spontaneity. In a moment of brave impulsivity, she books a week-long trip to Paris, choosing a charming Airbnb near Canal Saint-Martin. She arrives in the City of Light with an open heart and a sense of possibility. Her cozy apartment has a view of the canal, and her kind host Sylvie welcomes her warmly with fresh flowers and a handwritten guide to her favorite local spots. As Maya explores the enchanting neighborhoods, stumbles upon hidden gardens, meets fascinating people, and allows herself to get beautifully lost in winding streets, she discovers a version of herself she never knew existed - confident, adventurous, and fully alive.
