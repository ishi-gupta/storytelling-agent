Genre: Contemporary romance with corporate mystery/suspense

Time: Present day, six months (March–August), spanning pre–Series D diligence, Pulse beta, launch week, and post-launch fallout 
Theme: Trust vs. ambition; ethics in data; love under professional conflict; whistleblowing; power, secrecy, and work-life boundaries; how “industry standard” becomes cover for quiet fraud 
Tone: Grounded, tense, intimate; no melodrama, no fairy-tale wins 
Point of View: Alternating close third (Nora Patel, Eli Park)

Premise:  - RelayWorks preps a Series D and a flagship launch for “Pulse,” an analytics layer mining calendar/email metadata to surface “organizational drag.” A marquee case study with Metreon Logistics claims 28% productivity gains in 60 days. - Nora spots a hidden service called Orion pinging a backdoor API that “enriches” client data with third-party datasets. Priya flags that Metreon’s gains are statistically impossible without tampering.
